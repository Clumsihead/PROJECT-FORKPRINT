# Forkprint Vision

Forkprint is a privacy-first, local-first Android app that automatically builds a personal food memory. It is closer to "Google Photos for food memories" than a restaurant recommendation product.

## Product Principles

1. **Offline First**: the app must remain useful without internet access.
2. **AI Enhanced**: AI may enrich memories, search, summaries, and recommendations, but must never be required for core flows.
3. **Privacy by Default**: user food history is personal; local storage is the default and accounts are not required.

## MVP Experience

The MVP should feel elegant, minimal, personal, private, and calm. It should support:

- Automatic restaurant visit detection
- Restaurant database
- Visit history centered around memories
- Timeline
- Ratings
- Notes
- Search
- Local analytics
- Maps/Places integration where available

## Domain North Star

**Visit is the central object.**

Restaurant is a location. Visit is a memory. The app should read and feel like a food memory journal, not a business directory.
